Activity Log - Offline-First Daily Tracker

Activity Log is a lightweight, privacy-focused Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to track your daily habits, development hours, and personal milestones without requiring a constant internet connection. Your data never leaves your device.

Features

  • 100% Offline Support
  • No user accounts required
  • Local-first database via IndexedDB
  • Export functionality to PDF and JSON
